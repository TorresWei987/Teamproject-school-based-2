Proposed student experience

Lion's Guide will help students find reviewed handbook answers through a curated question bank. Students can type in their own words, but the website will suggest standard questions instead of answering free-form input directly. No AI service is required for this approach.

  1. A student types a question or a few words into the search box.
  2. The website shows up to five question-bank suggestions under 'Are you asking about...'. Alternative wording and common spelling mistakes help find suggestions.
  3. The student must select one standard question before an answer appears. Pressing Enter without a selection shows suggestions, not an answer or a guessed PDF page.
  4. The answer shows a short explanation, applicable conditions and exceptions, original handbook excerpts, and links to the cited PDF pages.
  5. Every suggestion list includes 'None of these match my question'. Students can browse categories or submit a question anonymously.
  6. Submitted questions enter a team review queue. They do not become public questions or answers automatically.

Editorial and matching rules

  • Publish only answers that the team has checked against the handbook and reviewed for conditions and exceptions.
  • Keep the selected question visible beside its answer so students can check that it matches their situation.
  • Question suggestions are possible matches, not a claim that the website understands every free-form sentence.
  • Do not treat an unmentioned situation as permission. Explain when the handbook does not provide a clear answer.
  • Keep alternative wording specific to each question. Do not treat all questions containing 'phone', 'late', or 'AI' as the same intent.
  • Provide category browsing and 'None of these match my question' when matching is weak or irrelevant.
  • Do not require names or email addresses on the new-question form. Do not promise individual replies. Check the chosen form service's metadata and retention behavior before describing submissions as fully anonymous.
  • Review the question bank when the school changes a cited policy. Keep a source reference and review date for each published answer.
  • All student-facing interface text, standard questions, and answers will be in English.
Review scenarios
  • A hoodie question should suggest the approved sweatshirt question, not an unrelated dress-code paragraph.
  • A vague phone question should offer separate school-use and storage questions without showing an answer first.
  • A phone-repair question, a missed-flight question, and an AI-pizza question must not automatically open school-policy answers.
  • A question with no suitable match must still offer category browsing and anonymous submission.
  • Exact-boundary and contradictory-source questions must remain on hold until the school clarifies them.

DRESS-01Draft · team review

Can I wear a hoodie or sweatshirt at school?

Only approved options are listed for normal school days: an approved OCS athletic, spirit, or leadership sweatshirt over an Oaks polo or Oxford. Juniors and seniors may also wear a qualifying college sweatshirt over an Oaks polo or Oxford.

Conditions and exceptions

  • A college sweatshirt must be a solid color and clearly display the college or university name.
  • The college-sweatshirt option is stated for juniors and seniors.
  • A sweatshirt does not replace the required polo or Oxford underneath.
  • Special non-uniform dress requires Office of Student Life permission; follow event-specific instructions.

Which shirts and colors are allowed for the normal school uniform?

Wear a school-approved polo or Oxford shirt with the OCS logo. Polo colors are white, cardinal, black, and gray. Oxford shirts may be white only.

Conditions and exceptions

  • Students must always wear a school-approved shirt.
  • Only the top button of an Oxford shirt may be unbuttoned.
  • White or black short-sleeved undershirts may be worn only underneath a polo or Oxford.
  • Follow approved instructions for any special non-uniform day.

What happens the first time I break the uniform rules?

The first offense includes a warning, but you must change into school uniform or wait for a parent to bring suitable uniform items before returning to class. A parent receives an automated message.

Conditions and exceptions

  • Students who do not follow uniform guidelines are sent to the Office of Student Life.
  • Each offense is recorded in the student's conduct section in AXIS.
  • Repeated violations have additional consequences; the first-offense answer does not cover that escalation schedule.

ATTENDANCE-01Draft · team review

How should my parent or guardian report an absence?

A parent or guardian should report the absence in AXIS, the preferred method. The handbook also lists the Attendance Office email for reporting after an absence has occurred. Absences must be cleared within 72 hours.

Conditions and exceptions

  • Parents should inform the school ahead of time when possible.
  • The listed email is agreason@oakschristian.org; check that this contact is still current before publication.
  • A student cannot rely on reporting the absence personally instead of parent or guardian communication.
  • An unreported absence can affect credit for assignments and assessments.

When is arriving late counted as an absence instead of a tardy?

The handbook links the attendance classification to how many minutes late you arrive. Its wording at exactly 15 minutes is inconsistent, so the Attendance Office needs to confirm that boundary before this answer is published.

Conditions and exceptions

  • Page 65 instead says 15 minutes or more is an unexcused absence.
  • Page 63 says the Attendance Office can determine whether an absence is parent reported. Do not promise that being parent reported removes all attendance or academic consequences.

How do I get permission to leave school early for an appointment?

A parent must report the reason and departure time in AXIS at least two hours ahead, or release may be delayed. Do not leave before the Attendance Office verifies parental permission.

Conditions and exceptions

  • Provide proof of the appointment when returning to school, such as a doctor's note.
  • A parent picking up a student must be verified at the front desk.
  • If you drive yourself, a parent must still inform the Attendance Office before release.
  • The handbook says students will not be released during lunch based on a parent phone call. Off-campus privileges follow separate rules.

Can missing a test affect my grade even if my parent reports the absence?

Yes. The Parent Reported/Excused Absence subsection says the first assessment-day absence receives a warning, and each subsequent assessment-day absence results in a 15% assessment penalty. Exceptions require administration approval.

  • The same section has makeup deadlines and additional rules for repeatedly missed AP or Honors assessments. Confirm your actual situation with the teacher and school administration.
  • The source contains two differently worded makeup-deadline rules. This answer does not promise an automatic five-day extension.

The general rule prohibits student cell-phone use throughout the school day, stated as 8:30 a.m. to 3:15 p.m., both indoors and outdoors. It is not limited to class time.

INTEGRITY-01Draft · team review

Can I use ChatGPT or another AI tool for homework or an assignment?

Do not present AI-assisted or software-generated work as your own authentic work without teacher approval. The handbook's AI overview also says AI used in the generative process must be acknowledged through citation.

Conditions and exceptions

  • Confirm the teacher's instructions for the actual assignment; do not assume permission carries over to other work.
  • Citation does not replace the teacher-approval requirement.
  • The overview says Large Language Model use should be primarily reserved for junior- and senior-level classes; it does not give automatic permission based on grade level.
  • The PDF links a fuller AI Philosophy and Guidelines document. Its detailed permissions have not been incorporated into this draft.

Is copying homework or letting a friend copy my work an academic-integrity violation?

Yes. Copying homework or classwork is listed as cheating. Allowing someone else to copy is listed as facilitating academic dishonesty, and the handbook says the helper is equally responsible for the violation.

INTEGRITY-06Draft · team review

Do I still need to cite a source if I put its ideas into my own words?

Yes. Summarizing or paraphrasing another person's ideas without acknowledging the source is listed as plagiarism. Rewriting the wording does not remove the need to credit the idea's source.

Conditions and exceptions

  • Directly using another writer's words also requires quotation marks.
  • Include a bibliography or works-cited page when the assignment requires one.
  • Ask the teacher which citation format to use if the assignment instructions do not specify it.
